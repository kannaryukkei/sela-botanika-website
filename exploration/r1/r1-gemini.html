Practical Details

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers on how stock, pricing, delivery, and workshops work in this studio concept.

How do stock and specific plant availability work?

Because living plants grow and arrive in small batches, we do not maintain a live online SKU catalog or guarantee that a specific cultivar is sitting on the shelf on a given day. Instead, we match your room’s light and care rhythm to healthy plants currently available at the studio.

Are the prices listed on this website final?

No. All ranges—plants (Rp35.000–450.000), pots/media/tools (Rp15.000–180.000), starter sets (Rp85.000–250.000), and workshops (Rp100.000–175.000 per participant)—are illustrative estimates only. Final pricing depends on plant size, pot material, and current studio availability, and must always be confirmed directly. Plant styling has no fixed price.

How is delivery or pickup handled?

To protect foliage and ceramic pots from transit stress, delivery options around Bandung or studio pickup arrangements are discussed individually when confirming an order or styling scope. We do not offer automated nationwide shipping or fixed delivery guarantees on this site.

When is the next beginner workshop, and how many seats are open?

We do not list fixed dates, hours, or seat capacities on this page. Workshops are organized on an inquiry-led basis for individuals, pairs, or small groups; reach out with your preferred days and topic interest to confirm upcoming sessions.

Can you guarantee a plant will survive in my room or is safe for pets?

No. Our Room-Fit Explorer offers practical, non-diagnostic starting suggestions based on light and routine, not a survival guarantee. We also do not provide veterinary pet-safety certifications, medical/air-purification claims, or specialist botanical pathology diagnosis.
